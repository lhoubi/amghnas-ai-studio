Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about typing, converting, and using the Tifinagh alphabet.

How does the online Tifinagh converter work? ▼
The converter translates words using the official IRCAM Neo-Tifinagh orthography. As you type in Tifinagh, Latin (Talatint), or Arabic, it computes correspondences in real time, handling both individual sounds and common consonant digraphs like gh (ⵖ), sh (ⵛ), and kh (ⵅ).
Can I copy and paste Tifinagh text into WhatsApp, Word, or Instagram? ▼
Yes. All output generated by amghnas uses standard international Unicode glyphs (U+2D30 to U+2D7F). Simply click the copy icon on any output box and paste the text into social media bios, messaging apps, graphic editing software, or documents.
What is the difference between Tifinagh, Talatint, and Tamazight? ▼
Tamazight is the language itself. Tifinagh is its indigenous geometric alphabet. Talatint is the standard Latin-based transcription system developed by linguists to write Tamazight with diacritics (like ḍ, ḥ, ɣ, ṛ, ṣ, ṭ, ẓ) for academic and educational clarity.
Why are Arabic vowels sometimes omitted in Arabic-to-Tifinagh conversion? ▼
Arabic typically does not write short vowels (harakat: fatha, damma, kasra). In contrast, Tifinagh explicitly writes each vowel (ⴰ, ⵓ, ⵉ, ⴻ). Therefore, converting unvocalized Arabic names directly may skip vowels. For the most accurate result, type your name using Talatint (Latin).
Which official standard does this platform follow? ▼
amghnas strictly adheres to the standard established by the Royal Institute of Amazigh Culture (IRCAM) and incorporated into the universal ISO 10646 / Unicode standard.
